## Strategic thesis

WiYW is a *plumber that leads with water quality* — the same dual position as JAG Plumbing
(21-yr plumber moving into water), but WiYW goes deeper on water. Two demand pools:

1. **Water-quality demand (differentiated, less competitive, higher margin):** softeners,
   filtration, RO, well treatment, PFAS. This is where WiYW wins. Well-water western PBC is
   thin on competition and high on pain.
2. **Plumbing demand (commodity, competitive, high intent):** emergency, water heater, drain,
   repipe, leak. WiYW captures this to fund and cross-sell into pool #1.

Regulatory wedge: federal PFAS MCL (4 ppt PFOA/PFOS) applies **only to public water systems**.
43M+ Americans on private wells have zero required testing/treatment. Western PBC well owners
are entirely on their own — a content + service opportunity no municipal utility covers.

## Keyword research process (repeatable, in Semrush)

1. **Seed:** enter `water softener palm beach`, `well water treatment`, `emergency plumber`,
   `water heater repair`, `whole house filter` into Keyword Magic Tool.
2. **Filter:** geo = Palm Beach County; intent = commercial/transactional; exclude national.
3. **Cluster:** group by service → one page per cluster, no cannibalization (one page owns
   `water softener` terms; don't split across two).
4. **Cross-check GSC:** after 3–4 weeks live, pull GSC Search Analytics for queries you're
   already impressing on. Prioritize pages where you're position 8–20 (striking distance).
5. **Gap analysis:** Semrush → competitors softwatertechs.com, aquasoftpalmbeach.com,
   waterlux.com, jagplumbinginc.com → find keywords they rank for that you don't → content backlog.
6. **Prioritize:** volume × (100 − keyword difficulty) × intent weight. Well-water terms get an
   intent bonus (thin competition, high ticket $3,500–$6,500).
