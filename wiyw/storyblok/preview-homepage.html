The difference

We test first. Then we fix what's actually there.

PBC water isn't the same street to street. Coastal homes fight hardness and chlorine; wells out west deal with iron, sulfur, and bacteria. Municipal PFAS rules don't cover private wells at all — if you're on a well, you're on your own. We test before we quote, so you buy exactly what your water needs.
